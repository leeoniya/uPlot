Soft limits, zero affinity, and hard limits

The precedence is hard > soft > zeroIf > pad. An omitted or null soft means no soft limit. There is no automatic soft zero. Both zeroIf and pad default to .1. The comparison charts use pad: .2 to make padding visible.

The first four charts share the same data: X values [0, 10] and initial Y values [5, 12]. The slider controls the second Y value. The first stays at 5. Cross -10 and 10 to compare soft limits with hard clipping. Move between 24 and 25 to see zeroIf: 0.25 activate.

12 25: zero-affinity threshold
