Live legend selection

This chart uses the log-scale demo's dataset: 24 hours of player counts for 12 Minecraft servers, from July 17–18, 2020.

The logarithmic Y axis separates clusters of smaller servers from outliers such as Hypixel. Zero counts use 1, as in the log-scale demo.

Choose a behavior: limit the legend, show the top servers, filter by cursor proximity, or rank all servers by player count.

Choose the legend position: top, right, bottom, or left. Limit only and Top servers use Per page: 5–50 in steps of 5, or 0 for all. The Time row does not consume the limit.

Nearby servers shows all visible servers until hovered, then applies the selected proximity threshold. Leaving the plot restores all visible servers.

Top servers uses the final sample when nothing is hovered, including its timestamp. A hover switches the ranking and displayed values to the cursor time.

Choose inline or table legend mode. The default is a table on the right. The controls are outside the legend.

Hover points automatically follow the final legend selection. Hidden series, missing values, and offscreen points remain hidden. Focus dimming is disabled.
