Adaptive log time axis

This demo adapts issue #1143. It compares compressed time with linear time.

The synthetic data covers 3,650 days (about 10 years) of p50 and p99 latency, with daily and weekly cycles. A latency regression affects the last 90 minutes.

Sample intervals depend on age:

  • Older than 90 days: 6 hours
  • 7 to 90 days: 1 hour
  • 6 hours to 7 days: 5 minutes
  • Last 6 hours: 10 seconds
Smaller τ Larger τ

The slider changes τ logarithmically.

All times use UTC. Zoom is disabled. The demo samples the wall clock on control updates, not continuously.
