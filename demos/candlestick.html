Dogecoin: Candlesticks, Hollow Candles & OHLC

Same data and renderer as the Grafana candlestick demo, with native uPlot axes, tooltips, and overlays.

Filled candles compare close to open. Hollow candles compare close to the previous close for color, and leave the body hollow when close is at least open.

Hover to compare values. Drag horizontally to zoom all charts; double-click to show all data. Times are UTC.
